Questions, not demands

What we are asking each other.

Nobody has voted on anything. These are the questions this page exists to test. If the answer is "don't care," the question dies here.

A raise that reaches everybody

Should the next contract have a percentage raise that covers everyone, including people paid above their classification's minimum, or should increases keep going to the minimums only?

Protection in plain words

Above-scale pay is protected today only by implication, in two general sentences. Should that protection be written as its own clause, in plain words, so it does not depend on interpretation?

Merit that stands on its own

Right now, if Encore gives its non-union staff no merit in a year, union members get none either, whatever their rating was. Should our merit hang on that decision, or stand on its own?

A ladder to Specialist

A Technician with a 1-Star certification is promoted to Lead automatically, and then the ladder stops. Should a certification path carry people toward Specialist, and should a promotion like that always come with a raise?

Seventh-day pay

When a show week runs long, should working a seventh day in a week carry its own premium?

Out times

Should out times be set, communicated, and honored, with something real happening when a show runs past them, instead of the night just ending whenever it ends?

Laptops for Specialists

Should the Specialist classifications come with a company laptop, so the job is not done on a personal machine?

Equal treatment

Should members in represented regions be treated the same as Encore staff in non-represented regions, on pay, benefits, and conditions generally, and not just on merit?

Scheduling, hours, and status are deliberately not on this list yet. If that is what actually bothers you, that is exactly what needs saying. It goes on the list when members raise it.
